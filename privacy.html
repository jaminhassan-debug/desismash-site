Privacy notice

DesiSmash by Chef Hass (“we”, “us”) is a small food business in Leicester. This explains what we do with your details.

Who we are

We are the data controller. Contact us at our WhatsApp number on the website.

What we collect and why

  • Free recipe download: your name and email, to send you the recipe. Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in providing what you asked for.
  • Offers and weekend specials: only if you tick the box. Lawful basis: your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by replying “stop” or emailing us, and we will remove you.
  • Orders: name, mobile, address (for delivery), email (optional) and your order, to prepare and deliver it. Lawful basis: performing your order. Orders come to us on WhatsApp.
  • Delivery postcode check: your postcode is sent to the postcodes.io service to work out the distance. No name or other detail is sent with it.

Who sees your details

Only us and the tools we use to run the business: our form provider (which stores sign-ups), WhatsApp (orders) and our email sender. We never sell your details.

How long we keep them

Order details for up to 12 months for our own records. Marketing sign-ups until you unsubscribe. Recipe-only sign-ups for up to 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, object to how we use them, or withdraw consent. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It stores your basket in your own browser so it is still there if you come back.
