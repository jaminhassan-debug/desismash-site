The concept

Product

Double smash burgers, 10 builds, plus a vegetarian option. Skin-on fries, loaded sides and five house sauces.

USP

Every burger comes as Original (classic American smash) or Desi (chilli, coriander, ginger-garlic, garam masala), in beef or chicken. That gives 40 combinations from one assembly line.

Founder story

Chef Hass, 25 years in professional kitchens working with spice. Cooks every order personally.

Hours

Friday, Saturday, Sunday, 5:00–10:30 pm. Thursday is prep day.

Why Leicester works: a large Halal and South Asian food culture, two universities (Leicester and DMU) and plenty of family delivery demand. Established smash and curry competitors are common, so the Desi fusion is the point of difference. Five Akhis is the local benchmark: strong brand story, loaded fries, short-form video, premium packaging, but at £10–£14 a meal. DesiSmash undercuts at £6.99–£8.99 and leads on chef authority.

Scope decision: launch with burgers only. No Bain Marie, no cooked-inventory risk, no spill risk on delivery. Curries (Korma, Tikka Masala, Madras, Bhuna, Garlic Chilli Jalfrezi from a 10L base gravy) come in once volume justifies them.

The 10 builds

1 The OG Smash

Bottom bun: 15ml OG house sauce. 3 dill pickle slices, 10g diced white onion. Patty, American cheese, patty, American cheese. Top: 10ml OG sauce.

2 The Oklahoma Onion

Bottom bun: 15ml mustard mayo. Press 20g razor-thin yellow onion into the raw patties before smashing. Two onion patties, American cheese on each.

3 The Naga Firecracker

Bottom bun: 15ml naga chilli mayo. 4 pickled jalapeño slices. Two patties with pepper jack. Top: 10g crispy shallots, 5ml naga mayo drizzle.

4 The Garlic Chilli Crisp

Bottom bun: 15ml roasted garlic aioli. Shredded iceberg. Two patties with chilli-jack. Top: 1 tsp chilli crisp oil.

5 The Caramelized Stampede

Bottom bun: 15ml smoky BBQ mayo. Two patties with smoked cheddar. Top: 25g slow-cooked caramelised onion, 2 strips turkey bacon or beef bacon jam.

6 The Tikka Butter Melt

Bottom bun: 15ml mint-coriander chutney mayo. 10g sliced red onion. Two patties with Red Leicester or spiced cheese. Top: 1 tbsp warm makhani butter glaze.

7 Truffle & Wild Mushroom

Bottom bun: 15ml garlic aioli or truffle mayo. Baby spinach. Two patties with Swiss cheese. Top: 30g garlic-and-thyme sautéed mushrooms.

8 Tamarind & Bombay Street

Bottom bun: 15ml coriander lime sauce. Two patties with aged cheddar. Top: 1 tbsp tamarind chutney, 10g crushed papri crisp.

9 Smoked BBQ Pitmaster

Bottom bun: 15ml smoky BBQ glaze. 3 pickle slices. Two patties with aged cheddar. Top: 1 crispy beer-battered onion ring.

10 The Triple Threat

Bottom bun: 20ml OG sauce. 4 pickle slices, 15g diced white onion. Three 2.5 oz patties, American cheese on each. Top: 15ml OG sauce.

The Paneer Tikka Melt (veg) isn't in this build set. Its recipe still needs to be written, along with a marinade (yogurt, mustard oil, roasted cumin, Kashmiri chilli) and a 110g slab.

Griddle timings

  • Beef at 200–220°C: smash thin at once, 60–90 s on side 1 until dark lace edges form, scrape, flip, add cheese, 30 s on side 2.
  • Chicken: 2 min on side 1, flip, cheese, 60–90 s on side 2. Core must reach 75°C.

Pub targets near Glenfield (LE3)

  • Independent wet-led pubs, craft taprooms, rugby and cricket clubs. Gemini named The Nags Head and The Railway Inn (Station Road, Glenfield), plus pubs in Groby and Anstey. Confirm each one is currently open and food-free before approaching.

  • Pub residency schedule: Friday 5:00–10:00 pm, Saturday 4:00–10:00 pm, Sunday 4:00–9:00 pm. That is 16 service hours a week.
  • Speed target: 90 seconds or less per burger ticket, using a heavy 16–20mm steel plate (800–1200mm) kept above 200°C. A second smash line helps at peak.
  • Site line: high-CFM stainless canopy with baffle filters, two 12L fryers, 2-door prep fridge with topping wells, dual sinks plus a hot-water handwash basin.
  • Pitch fee: budget 10–12% of sales or a flat daily rate.
  • 100-burger prep batch: about 6 kg beef original, 7.5 kg beef desi, 4 kg chicken, 300 cheese slices, 1 kg each of pickles, white, red and yellow onions.
  • Suppliers (Leicester): Meat Boys Supply Co. for halal 80/20 mince; Adams Farm Foods for chicken and lamb; Booker (Meridian, LE3) for brioche, cheese, boxes, bags; 24-can slabs for drinks at 38–42p each.
  • Allergen matrix: gluten, sesame, dairy, mustard, egg, nuts where used.

Compliance checklist

Registration

Register the food business with your local council at least 28 days before trading. Free, and triggers an inspection. Glenfield sits under Blaby District; Leicester city addresses use Leicester City Council.

Food safety

Food hygiene certificate (get a replacement from the original provider or retake Level 2, about £20). Safer Food Better Business pack and daily temperature logs.

Insurance

Public and product liability, £5M cover, about £150–£250 a year. Tell your landlord or mortgage lender if cooking at home.

If converting a garage

Full planning (change of use), building control, grease trap and trade effluent consent, business rates declaration. Blaby planning fee about £550.
