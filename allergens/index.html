Allergens and nutrition estimates

All our burgers contain gluten, milk, egg, mustard and sesame. Tell us about any allergy when you order, and Chef Hass will confirm on WhatsApp what is safe for you. We cook on shared equipment, so we cannot guarantee any item is free from an allergen.

Each item lists the allergens it contains as an ingredient, out of the 14 that must be declared in the UK. The Chicken Desi patty also contains milk (butter).
