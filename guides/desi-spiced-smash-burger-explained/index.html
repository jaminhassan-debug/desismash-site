Desi spiced smash burger: what's in the patty?

By DesiSmash by Chef Hass · Nov 2026 · 3 min read

A Desi spiced smash burger is a smash burger whose patty is mixed with fresh chilli, coriander, ginger-garlic and ground spices before it hits the griddle, so the flavour is built into the meat rather than only sitting in the sauce.

Chef Hass has cooked with spice for 25 years, and DesiSmash is that experience in a smash burger. Every burger on the menu comes in two styles: Original, a classic American-style smash, or Desi, with the spiced patty. Here is what that actually means.

What goes into the Desi patty

  • Beef Desi: mixed with green chilli, coriander, ginger-garlic, cumin and garam masala, in two 2.5 oz patties.
  • Chicken Desi: chicken thigh mixed with mint, chaat masala and cumin, in two 2.5 oz patties.

The Original patties, in comparison, are seasoned simply with salt and pepper on the griddle, in two 2 oz patties, so the beef or chicken itself stays centre stage.

Why spice the patty itself?

Many of the flavour compounds in spices dissolve in fat. When a spiced patty hits a very hot griddle, the fat renders out, carries those flavours with it and fries the edges, so the spices bloom right where the crust forms. That is why a Desi patty tastes toasted and fragrant rather than raw and dusty. Adding fresh chilli, coriander and ginger-garlic gives brightness on top of the warmth from cumin and garam masala.

Is Desi the same as hot?

No. Desi means spiced, not necessarily hot. Heat depends on the burger you choose. Here is a simple guide to the menu:

  • No heat: The OG Smash, The Oklahoma Onion, Smoked BBQ Pitmaster, Caramelized Stampede, Truffle & Wild Mushroom and The Triple Threat.
  • Mild warmth: Tikka Butter Melt, Tamarind & Bombay Street and Paneer Tikka Melt.
  • Medium heat: Garlic Chilli Crisp.
  • Hot: The Naga Firecracker, with naga chilli mayo, pickled jalapeños and crispy shallots.

Choosing the Desi patty adds spice to the meat, while the burger you pick decides how much chilli heat you get.
