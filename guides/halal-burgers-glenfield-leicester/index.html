Halal burgers in Glenfield and Leicester: order guide

By DesiSmash by Chef Hass · Nov 2026 · 2 min read

DesiSmash cooks halal beef and chicken smash burgers for Glenfield and Leicester every Friday, Saturday and Sunday from 5 pm. Pre-order a 30-minute slot on the website and collect for free. There is no pork or alcohol in our kitchen.

Update: DesiSmash is collection only for now. Delivery through the delivery apps is coming soon. Where this guide mentions delivery, the details may change when delivery opens.

If you live in Glenfield, Braunstone, Kirby Muxloe, Groby, Anstey, Leicester Forest East or Leicester itself, here is how to order halal smash burgers from us.

When we are open

  • Friday, Saturday and Sunday: 5:00 pm to 10:30 pm, with last orders at 10:00 pm.
  • Other days: we prep sauces and spice blends, so we do not take orders.

What is halal about our burgers

Our beef and chicken come from a halal supplier. There is no pork and no alcohol in our kitchen, and the turkey bacon is halal. We do not hold our own halal certificate. See are DesiSmash burgers halal? for the full answers.

What to order first

  • Classic: The OG Smash with a Beef Original patty.
  • A little spice: The Tikka Butter Melt with a Desi patty.
  • Big heat: The Naga Firecracker.
  • Very hungry: The Triple Threat, with three 2.5 oz patties.
  • Vegetarian: The Paneer Tikka Melt.

How to order in four steps

  1. Build your basket on the menu. Choose Original or Desi, beef or chicken.
  2. Pick a slot on Friday, Saturday or Sunday.
  3. Send your order on WhatsApp.
  4. Wait for Chef Hass to message you. Reply YES and your order is booked. You pay when you collect.

We are collection only for now, and collection is always free. Delivery through the delivery apps is coming soon. For more local details read smash burgers in Glenfield and how to pre-order.

Allergies

All our burgers contain gluten, milk and egg, and we cook on shared equipment. Tell us about any allergy when you order.
