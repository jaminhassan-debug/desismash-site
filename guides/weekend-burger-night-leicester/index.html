Weekend burger night in Leicester: Friday, Saturday or Sunday?

By DesiSmash by Chef Hass · Nov 2026 · 2 min read

DesiSmash is open for burger night every Friday, Saturday and Sunday from 5:00 pm to 10:30 pm, with last orders at 10:00 pm. Pick the night that suits you, choose a 30-minute slot, and we will smash your burgers fresh for then.

A weekend burger night is one of the easiest ways to make an evening feel special. You can plan it in a few minutes, and everyone gets what they like. Here is how to make the most of it in Leicester, whichever night you pick.

Choose your night

Friday: start the weekend

Friday is the first night we cook each week. It suits an end-of-week treat, whether that is a quiet night in or friends coming over. Order a few burgers, a loaded fries to share, and some dip pots.

Saturday: friends or family

Saturday evenings are good for a bigger order. Build a mixed basket, one for each taste: a classic, a spicy one, a smoky one and a vegetarian. See ordering for a group or family.

Sunday: an easy dinner

Sunday is a good night to skip cooking. Order early evening, collect or have it delivered, and finish the weekend with something you did not have to make.

Choose your slot

  • Earlier (from 5 pm): good for families and an early dinner.
  • Mid evening: good for friends and a relaxed night.
  • Later (up to last orders at 10 pm): good for a late bite.

Slots are 30 minutes long, and the earliest slot available on the day is about 45 minutes after you order. Booking earlier in the day gives you the widest choice.

Build the order

For a night in for two

  • Two burgers of different styles, such as The OG Smash and the Tikka Butter Melt.
  • Desi Loaded Smash Fries to share.
  • Two cold cans.

For a family

  • A mix of classics for the less adventurous.
  • Seasoned skin-on fries in sea salt, which is simple and popular.
  • Dip pots in different flavours for everyone to try.

For friends

  • One hot burger for the brave, the Naga Firecracker, and a mint-coriander dip to cool down. See our heat guide.
  • Naga Cheese Fries and Desi Loaded Smash Fries in the middle.
  • A vegetarian option for anyone who needs it: the Paneer Tikka Melt.

Make it smooth

  • Choose collection or delivery. Collection is free, and delivery is available within 3 miles of our kitchen.
  • Send your order on WhatsApp and wait for Chef Hass to reply, because your order is only confirmed then.
  • Put names and allergies in the notes box. All our burgers contain gluten, milk and egg.
  • Set the table first. Burgers are best eaten hot.
