Smash burgers made for Glenfield and Leicester

DesiSmash is a small, chef-led kitchen in Leicester run by Chef Hass, who has spent 25 years cooking with spice. Every burger is a double smash on a butter-toasted brioche bun, apart from The Triple Threat, which has three 2.5 oz patties. Every order is smashed fresh on a very hot griddle, so the crust is crisp when it reaches you.

We are a pre-order kitchen rather than a sit-down restaurant. You choose your food and a time slot online, we confirm your order on WhatsApp, and you either collect or have it delivered.

Collection and delivery

Collection is always free. When you order, pick a collection slot and we confirm the details when we reply on WhatsApp.

Delivery is available within 3 miles of our kitchen and is priced by distance, with the closest addresses paying the least. If you live in or near Glenfield, enter your postcode in the delivery fee calculator on the home page to see whether you are in range and what the fee is before you order. Areas close to Glenfield that people often ask about include Braunstone, Kirby Muxloe, Groby, Anstey and Leicester Forest East, so check your postcode to be sure.

What to order first

  • New to smash burgers: The OG Smash with a Beef Original patty. American cheese, OG house sauce, pickles and diced onion.
  • Want some spice: The Tikka Butter Melt with a Desi patty. Creamy, tangy and gently warm.

How to order in four steps

  1. Build your order on the menu. Choose Original or Desi, beef or chicken.
  2. Choose collection or delivery, and pick a Friday, Saturday or Sunday slot.
  3. Send your order on WhatsApp, and keep the website open. You will see an order confirmation on screen.
  4. Wait for our reply. Your order is confirmed when Chef Hass replies on WhatsApp. You pay on collection or delivery.

Quick answers

Do you deliver to Glenfield?
