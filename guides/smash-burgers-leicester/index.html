What is a smash burger?

A smash burger is made by pressing a ball of mince flat onto a very hot griddle so it cooks into a thin patty with a crisp, browned crust. The thin patties are usually stacked two or three high, with cheese melted between them. If you are new to the idea, start with what is a smash burger? and smash burger vs thick burger.

What to look for in a smash burger in Leicester

  • A visible crust. The edges of the patty should be dark, crisp and lacy, not pale and flat.
  • Cooked to order. A smash patty is best straight off the griddle. Ask whether burgers are made to order.
  • Melted cheese. The cheese should be melted into the patty, not sitting on top like a slice.
  • A toasted bun. A buttered, toasted bun keeps the burger from going soggy. See why brioche suits a smash burger.
  • Sauce you can taste. Sauce should balance the richness, not drown the crust.
  • Clear allergen information. Any food business should tell you what is in its food and be happy to answer questions.
