How to check a burger is halal: 8 questions to ask

By DesiSmash by Chef Hass · Nov 2026 · 2 min read

To check a burger is halal, ask whether the meat is halal and who supplies it, whether there is pork or alcohol in the kitchen, and whether the bacon, cheese and sauces are suitable. A good seller answers happily. Here are the eight questions, with our answers.

Not every burger menu that says halal means the same thing. These questions take two minutes and tell you what you are eating.

The eight questions

1. Is the beef halal?

Ask for a clear yes or no and the supplier. At DesiSmash, our beef is halal, from a halal supplier.

2. Is the chicken halal?

Some places serve halal beef but not chicken, so check both. Our chicken is halal too.

3. Who is the supplier, and who certifies it?

If a certifier such as HMC or HFA matters to you, ask. DesiSmash does not hold its own halal certificate. Message Chef Hass on WhatsApp for supplier details. See halal vs HMC explained.

4. Is there pork in the kitchen?

There is no pork in our kitchen.

5. Is there alcohol in the sauces or cooking?

Some sauces and glazes use alcohol or vinegars made with it. There is no alcohol in our sauces or cooking.

6. Is the bacon halal?

Burger menus often carry bacon. Our turkey bacon on the Caramelized Stampede is halal.

7. What about the cheese and sauces?

Some people check for animal rennet in cheese and gelatine in sauces. Our cheese is made without animal rennet and our sauces contain no gelatine.

8. What about allergies?

All our burgers contain gluten, milk and egg, and we cook on shared equipment, so we cannot guarantee any item is free from an allergen. Tell us when you order.
