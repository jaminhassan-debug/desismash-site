Ordering smash burgers for a group or family

By DesiSmash by Chef Hass · Nov 2026 · 3 min read

To order smash burgers for a group, put everything in one basket, choose one Friday, Saturday or Sunday slot, add a mix of burgers and sides to share, and use the notes box for allergies or special requests. You get one order, one delivery fee and one slot.

Burger night works best when everyone gets something they like. Here is how to plan a group or family order from DesiSmash in Leicester or Glenfield, without the stress.

Step 1: count your people and appetites

  • Normal appetites: one double smash burger each. Our burgers are double smashes.
  • Hungry people: add fries to the meal, or share a loaded fries between two.
  • Very hungry: The Triple Threat.
  • Children or lighter eaters: sharing one burger and a portion of fries between two is often plenty.

Step 2: mix the burgers

Everyone has a different taste. A good group order has variety:

  • One crowd-pleaser: The OG Smash is rarely a bad choice.
  • One for spice lovers: Tikka Butter Melt or Garlic Chilli Crisp.
  • One bold one: Naga Firecracker for the brave.
  • One vegetarian: Paneer Tikka Melt. Read our vegetarian guide.
  • One sweet and smoky: Smoked BBQ Pitmaster or Caramelized Stampede.

If you are not sure what people will like, send them our guide to which smash burger to order first.

Step 3: add sides to share

Sides are the easiest thing to share:

  • Seasoned skin-on fries in your choice of masala dust, peri-peri or sea salt.
  • Desi Loaded Smash Fries or Naga Cheese Fries, which are great in the middle of the table.
  • Dip pots, because everyone likes to try each other's sauces.
  • Cold cans: Coke, Diet Coke, Fanta, Rubicon Mango or water.

Step 4: choose collection or delivery

  • Collection is always free and suits when one person is going out anyway.
  • Delivery is available within 3 miles of our kitchen, with the fee shown before you order. Because it is one order, you pay one delivery fee, not one each. See how delivery works.

Step 5: handle allergies and special requests

Put everything in the notes box: who has an allergy, who wants no onions, who wants mild. We read every note. Please remember that all our burgers contain gluten, milk and egg, and we cannot guarantee any item is free from an allergen, so tell us about any allergy when you order.

Tips for a smooth burger night

  • Order early. Booking your slot earlier in the day gives you the widest choice of times.
  • Be ready on time. Get plates and drinks out so the burgers are eaten hot.
  • Put names in the notes. Writing who gets which burger stops arguments at the table.
  • Check opening hours. We cook Friday, Saturday and Sunday, 5 pm to 10:30 pm, with last orders at 10 pm.

A sample order for four

  1. 2 x The OG Smash (one Beef Original, one Chicken Original)
  2. 1 x Tikka Butter Melt (Chicken Desi)
  3. 1 x Paneer Tikka Melt
  4. 2 x seasoned fries (one masala dust, one sea salt)
  5. 1 x Desi Loaded Smash Fries to share
  6. Dip pots and cans for everyone
