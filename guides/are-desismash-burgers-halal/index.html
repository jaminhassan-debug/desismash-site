Are DesiSmash burgers halal?

By DesiSmash by Chef Hass · Nov 2026 · 2 min read

Yes, our beef and chicken are halal, bought from a halal supplier. There is no pork and no alcohol in our kitchen. DesiSmash does not hold its own halal certificate, and we say so plainly, so you can decide for yourself.

Update: DesiSmash is collection only for now. Delivery through the delivery apps is coming soon. Where this guide mentions delivery, the details may change when delivery opens.

Customers in Leicester often ask about halal before they order. Here are the questions we hear most, answered straight.

Our meat

Are your burgers halal?

Our beef and chicken are halal, supplied by a halal supplier. Message Chef Hass on WhatsApp if you would like details about the supplier.

Is the chicken halal too?

Yes. Our chicken thigh patties are halal, in both Original and Desi.

Is DesiSmash HMC certified?

No. DesiSmash does not hold its own halal certificate and does not claim to be HMC certified. Our beef and chicken come from a halal supplier. Read halal vs HMC explained to see what certification means.

The rest of the burger

Do you use pork or alcohol?

No. There is no pork and no alcohol in our kitchen.

Is the turkey bacon halal?

Yes. The turkey bacon on the Caramelized Stampede is halal.

What about the cheese and sauces?

Our sauces contain no alcohol or gelatine, and our cheese is made without animal rennet. Our sauces and cheeses contain milk, egg, mustard and gluten, so tell us about any allergy when you order.

Is the vegetarian burger halal-friendly?

The Paneer Tikka Melt is vegetarian and contains no meat. It is cooked on the same griddle as the meat burgers.
