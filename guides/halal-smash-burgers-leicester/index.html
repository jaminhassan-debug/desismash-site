If you are searching for halal burgers in Leicester, you probably want three things: meat you can trust, a kitchen that respects your diet, and a burger that actually tastes good. This guide explains what we do, so you can decide for yourself.

What is a halal smash burger?

A smash burger is a thin patty pressed onto a very hot griddle so it forms a crisp, browned crust. A halal smash burger is the same burger made with halal meat. See what is a smash burger? if you are new to the style.

What is halal at DesiSmash

  • Beef and chicken: both come from a halal supplier.
  • No pork: there is no pork in our kitchen.
  • No alcohol: there is no alcohol in our sauces or our cooking.
  • Turkey bacon: the turkey bacon on the Caramelized Stampede is halal.
  • Sauces and cheese: no alcohol or gelatine in our sauces, and cheese made without animal rennet.

We are honest about what we do not claim. DesiSmash does not hold its own halal certificate. Our meat comes from a halal supplier, and you are welcome to message Chef Hass on WhatsApp for the details. Read more in halal vs HMC: what halal burgers really mean.
